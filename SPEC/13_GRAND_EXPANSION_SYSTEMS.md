# SPEC 13: Grand Expansion Systems

**Status: design proposal, post-v1; not ratified or implemented.** This document defines candidate systems and acceptance criteria, not evidence of balance or a release commitment. SPEC 01?12 and the repository's core rules remain authoritative on conflict. In particular, the starter choice, Sentry Scout, and two-cat Alpha recipe below require explicit canon amendments before implementation. No existing medal, round, enemy hierarchy, unlock, or balance gate changes merely by adding this document.

## 1. Boundaries and shared rules

The expansion is free: no ads, payments, premium track, paid random rewards, or paid access. Fishcoin is earned in play and buys only cosmetics and Tricks. All competitive and cooperative play is optional and post-v1; the single-player game remains independently playable. Standard modes, their authored rounds, prices, progression and Bare Paw rules remain in SPEC 01?12. Expansion rewards must never be required to clear standard maps or earn standard medals. Custom maps and special modes award no standard map medals unless separately certified under the existing fairness gates.

Simulation uses 30 ticks per second, seeded randomness, discrete replayable inputs, content-defined numbers, stable ordering, and no scene tree, real time, networking, or presentation calls. Each ruleset has a version and content digest. Presentation supplies input and renders output; it cannot resolve gameplay. Multiplayer cannot assume that the existing sim already uses cross-platform fixed-point arithmetic: that property and identical results across supported builds are prerequisites to release. Time in UI and transport is not simulation time.

"106 maps" is the planned six original maps plus the 100-map Ward Atlas, not a claim that all have shipped. Twelve mouse coats and five rats are 17 named enemy types, not necessarily 17 linear tiers. All modes inherit coat immunities, pop overflow, actual HP Kibble, leak RBE, crosspath limits and the central Matriarch immunity. All simulation-facing quantities below are proposed content values and require tests before release.

## 2. First contact: five-minute triad

1. **Meet**: a skippable three-panel, accessible-text vignette introduces the Great Mouse Breach at North Fish Pier. Panel art is presentation only; replay never waits on it.
2. **Place**: introduce Pip, range and track placement on Sunny Gutter. Give contextual prompts with an option to dismiss or replay them. Preserve the authored Sunny rounds, starting resources and level progression.
3. **Learn pierce**: show a split and the effect of separate pierce using the existing coats, followed by the existing round-five upgrade prompt. Explain immunity by shape and text, not color alone.

**Decision required ? hero draft.** A choice of Mittens or Duchess at first contact, with the other unlocked at level 7, conflicts with SPEC 06's unconditional Duchess level-7 unlock and the established Mittens starter. Keep the existing unlocks until a migration and amendment to SPEC 05/06 are ratified; any tutorial-only loan must not unlock a hero or change standard matches. Do not describe Mittens as a needle specialist: use the actual kits in SPEC 05.

**Decision required ? Sentry Scout.** An armored round-five mouse splitting into 15 mice conflicts with the authored round and early Iron timing. Do not change Sunny or add an eighteenth named enemy until its coat, RBE, immunity, tutorial placement, rewards and regression results are approved. A separate opt-in scripted teaching encounter is a possible prototype, not an authorized change to standard round 5.

## 3. Clowder Patrol: two-player co-op

Each participant owns cats, their upgrades and a separate Kibble wallet. Map content supplies two disjoint placement regions; boundary points use the lower player ID as a deterministic tie-break. Only the owner can place, upgrade, sell, target or synthesize their cats. An owned aura or status can affect a valid target across the boundary; ordinary range, targeting and immunity apply. A hero per player would conflict with the one-hero-per-match rule, so the match has one shared hero: player 0 chooses and controls it. Both players must accept starting the next round; feat choices belong to the cat owner and resolve between rounds before the next start.

For each original spawn event at tick `t`, produce the original at `t` and a duplicate at `t + 5` ticks. This is exactly double count and approximates 0.15 seconds at 30 Hz (5/30 s); IDs and tie ordering are stable. Child spawns follow normal pop rules, not this duplication. Set shared lives to `ceil(base_lives * 3 / 2)`; therefore Bare Paw remains one or two only if explicitly treated as a separate co-op ruleset, never as a standard Bare Paw medal. Each wallet begins with the mode's normal starting Kibble. Award pop income to the owner of the damage source for HP actually removed, including overflow; a persistent status retains its originating owner. Leaks pay nothing. Divide ownerless round-end income equally, with any odd Kibble to player 0. Content must define ownership for all other income and costs before release; no unowned income is silently discarded.

Shared lives reach zero: both lose. Clear the final round: both win the co-op result, not a single-player medal. Disconnect pauses at the last agreed tick; reconnect with identical build/content and verified snapshot plus action history, otherwise terminate without a win. Both may agree to end. No automatic AI takeover or unilateral continue. Never grant rewards twice after reconnect.

### Lockstep contract

SPEC 11 defines framing and validation. Each player sends an explicit frame, including empty frames, for every simulated tick. Advance only when both frames are present, applying valid actions in `(player_id, sequence)` order. Illegal actions produce deterministic rejections and do not mutate state. Input delay is a negotiated integer number of ticks, identical for both players; transport latency never changes simulation rules. Round starts, pause votes and feat selections are actions; local UI speed is not. Hash state at agreed tick boundaries; halt on mismatch and retain diagnostics. Deterministic lockstep reduces traffic but does not supply NAT traversal, cheat prevention, secure identity, availability, or trivial P2P hosting. A relay or host service may be necessary. Do not advertise multiplayer until platform determinism, recovery, security and transport have been tested.

## 4. Alley Crawl expedition

A separate seeded run comprises 5?7 district nodes, with the count and directed acyclic graph generated once at run creation and saved. Each node offers one or more reachable next nodes; select only between cleared districts. A district references a certified map and a published round set. Its first district uses that ruleset's stated starting Kibble; later districts start with their stated base plus `floor(previous_remaining_kibble / 4)`. Do not carry placed cats, lives, temporary statuses, or in-match feats unless a future ruleset explicitly says so. Zero lives ends the run; completing the final district wins. No standard-map medals are awarded.

Generate offers from stable, sorted eligible IDs with a versioned seeded PRNG; store the seed, generator version, chosen nodes and drafts so save/reload cannot reroll. At each cleared non-final district offer three distinct eligible Relics, choose one, and optionally choose one visible Curse. A chosen relic or curse lasts until run end; duplicate IDs are ineligible. If fewer than three are eligible, show all remaining and never fabricate an option. Relic and curse effects must be deterministic sim modifiers, authored with stacking, targets, caps and ordering; a tooltip must disclose numerical effects. Golden Whisker and Catnip Satchel are placeholder names, not approved mechanics. Each curse's fixed Fishcoin bonus is granted once on completed run, subject to the content-defined cap; no reward for abandoning or replaying a claimed result.

A district receives zero to two distinct modifiers from a versioned set of 12. Dense Fog, Greased Cobbles, Ink Tide and Frenzy Clock are candidate names only. Their exact effects, eight remaining modifiers, incompatibilities, district waves, reward values and full node generation algorithm are release blockers: do not ship a mode relying on invented or missing content. Publish seeds and fixture outputs and prove every generated path has a valid encounter and bounded runtime.

## 5. Additional modes and creator tools

These are independent rulesets, never silent alterations of the five standard modes. Their authored content must specify starting resources, waves, progression eligibility, win/loss, action legality and RNG version. Mode-specific debug controls never affect normal matches.

- **Sandbox / Testing Lab:** infinite Kibble, arbitrary enemy and round spawner, enemy freeze, single tick step, damage-per-second measurement and terrain-mask overlays. Debug freeze is a global simulation pause, not a status on enemies, so Matriarch immunity remains intact. No Account XP, Mastery, Fishcoin, achievements, medals or persistent progression. Spawn, toggles and steps are replayed commands; counters report observed HP removed over elapsed sim ticks, with zero elapsed time displayed as unavailable.
- **Rat Gauntlet:** begin with 5,000 Kibble; fight Burrow, Bristle, Monarch, Dusk, then Matriarch. Advance after all enemies in a stage are cleared, with a between-stage build and feat window. Lives reaching zero loses; clearing Matriarch and children wins. Stage HP, timings, modifiers, income, lives and rewards require authored content and balance validation before release; normal immunity applies.
- **Alley Invaders:** the player spends a separate in-mode grain resource to send mice against deterministic AI cat defenses, attempting to leak enough remaining RBE into the Fish Vault before a content-defined deadline. AI uses sim state and seeded decisions; no Fishcoin purchases power. Spawn prices, grain income, AI loadouts, vault capacity and deadline are unresolved release blockers.
- **Puzzles:** 25 individually authored challenges specify map revision, legal cat inventory and quantities, fixed Kibble, rounds, allowed actions, objective and solution proof. No purchases outside inventory, random round changes or hidden starting resources. Clearing the published objective wins; losing all lives or exhausting a stated objective loses. Puzzle definitions and independently replayable solutions are required before claiming 25 exist. Candidate titles: Ink Paradox and Sniper's Narrow Alley.
- **Alley Duel:** two separate synchronized tracks, one player per defense, both using the lockstep contract. A legal send deducts the sender's Kibble, schedules an authored enemy on the opponent's track and permanently adds the send's published integer eco value to the sender's round-end income. A send is atomic; unaffordable or invalid sends do nothing. Zero lives loses; simultaneous zero on the same tick is a draw. Each player owns their defense and at most one hero on their own track; this is two separate matches, not two heroes in one match. Sending catalog, eco values, round schedule, starting resources, anti-stall limit and reward policy are release blockers. Competitive fairness requires identical permitted content and loadout rules.
- **Map Editor:** polyline tools, Land/Water/Perch/Blocker mask painter and decorative props export versioned JSON described in SPEC 11. Validate bounds, nonzero segments, entrances/exits, route clearance, masks, stable IDs, file size and resource limits before load; never execute imported scripts or fetch assets. Props have no simulation effect unless represented as a validated blocker. Share codes encode a bounded compressed map payload with version and checksum; reject corrupt or oversized codes. Checksums detect errors, not malicious input. Custom maps do not enter the official roster or grant official medals without the SPEC 12 fairness process.

## 6. Mastery, prestige and Alpha design

Mastery ranks 1?10 and their XP thresholds remain SPEC 06. Proposed ranks 11?20 confer cosmetics only, never combat stats, power, or progression shortcuts. Their thresholds and badges, stat frames and sound flairs must be published as content; existing XP must be preserved on migration. Account prestige can reset only voluntarily after confirmation and must return all spent Insight points immediately, preserving Account XP, levels and unlocks. A crest and cosmetic-only shop discount are candidates; discount applies only to cosmetics, never Tricks, and requires an authored floor and cap. Repeated prestige must not duplicate points or allow negative prices.

**Alpha decision required.** SPEC 04 requires three Tier-5 versions of the same type and Rank 10. The proposed two different Rank-10 cats, a Kibble synthesis price, one Alpha slot and named combinations are incompatible with it. Neither recipe is implemented or ratified by this document. A final proposal must define input ownership, tiers and positions, cost, inherited stats and feats, refunds, exact Alpha stats, crosspath/Tier-5 slot interaction, replay IDs and save migration. No Alpha may be necessary for Bare Paw; until approved it is unavailable everywhere.

Veteran feats: propose three new cards per launch cat, eligible only after round 80 and offered through the existing seeded between-round draft, not extra mid-round choices. Twenty-four card definitions, eligibility, stacking and balance are still required; the existing three feat thresholds and deterministic seed stay unchanged unless separately amended.

## 7. Narrative and character lore

Four proposed campaign acts cover the breach, Baron Salt's seafaring past, Sister Pebble's sanctuary and the rise of the Rat Matriarch. Their exact maps, dialogue, unlocks and outcomes need authored content. Dialogue is optional and skippable. A deterministic trigger references a named event and script ID; presentation renders a data-only list of speaker, emotion, line and optional SFX IDs. Scripts cannot change combat or block a tick; dialogue choices with mechanical consequences require explicit recorded actions and a separately approved ruleset. Text alternatives, subtitles and volume controls apply. Schema is in SPEC 11.

## 8. Ledger, collection and events

The Alley Ledger is a free 50-tier track; eligible Account XP earned after enrollment also advances its separate ledger XP without consuming Account XP. Thresholds, rewards, season ID and dates are published data; each tier is claimed at most once. Only cosmetics, audio and Fishcoin are permitted rewards. No premium tier, cash purchase or gameplay power. Each player can select an archived track to earn its remaining rewards, one active track at a time; season expiry never deletes earned or unclaimed progress. Offline clients use a signed or bundled published calendar for presentation and can continue archived tracks; wall clock must not enter simulation. Calendar trust and reconciliation are unresolved before online time-limited contracts ship.

Almanac reads the same content as sim for stats, immunity and multipliers; lore and cosmetic layered 2D views are presentation data, not a claim of 3D assets. Hide undiscovered lore if desired but do not hide combat-critical immunities. Lifetime dashboard records Kibble spent, actual RBE popped, compositions, wins and attempts per ward, with versioned event IDs and idempotent result commits; define efficiency formulas in content before display. Aggregate counters must not include sandbox.

At least 50 authored achievements may use Bronze, Silver, Gold and Onyx thresholds, with cosmetic titles; each needs a stable ID, metric, threshold, eligibility, reward and one-time claim state. Do not count retries or duplicate result commits twice. Harvest Feast, Winter Lantern Festival and Midnight Alley are candidate cosmetic rotations with challenge contracts; their dates, conditions and Fishcoin amounts require published content. Missed event cosmetics return through archived play or a Fishcoin cosmetic catalog, never a paid or permanently exclusive offer.

## 9. Verification and release gates

1. Run `npm test` and `npm run test:sunny`; preserve all nine existing gates. Test standard round scripts and the fresh-profile level-10 XP assertion unchanged.
2. Golden replay fixtures compare canonical state hashes per tick across repeated runs, supported platforms and builds; include simultaneous actions, invalid actions, disconnect and resync, status ownership, overflow and Matriarch immunity. Packet loss, delay, reordering and duplicate frames must not change outcomes; mismatch halts rather than granting rewards.
3. Validate all versioned JSON, ranges, references, duplicate IDs, resource limits, migrations and round trips. Fuzz map imports, corrupted share codes and hostile network payloads. A checksum is not authentication.
4. Simulate co-op economy and leak cases, expedition generation over a published seed corpus, each puzzle's solution, Duel symmetry and all proposed modifier combinations. Publish quantitative win-rate, duration and reward targets before declaring balance; successful tests cannot prove all seeds or players fair.
5. Re-run standard and Ward Atlas Bare Paw fairness gates with naturally available Mastery and without expansion power. Check ledger, prestige and event claims cannot duplicate rewards or grant power outside the Fishcoin boundary. Verify accessibility and offline/archive recovery manually.

Recommended prototype order, after the conflicting design decisions are ratified: (1) mode harness and isolated Sandbox, (2) expedition loop and content validation, (3) fixed-point determinism audit and lockstep, (4) map editor validation and export, (5) UI and presentation. This is a proposal, not approval to implement gameplay.
