# SPEC 08: Fishcoin, Tricks & The Shop

## 1. Fishcoin Economy & Monetization Philosophy

Fishcoin is the sole meta-currency in Clowder. It is earned exclusively through gameplay and is spent on active tactical Tricks and cosmetic enhancements.

### Zero-Monetization Canon
- **No Real-Money Purchases**: There are no in-app purchases, microtransactions, or premium currencies.
- **No Loot Boxes**: All items are purchased directly from a static catalog with clear prices.
- **No Pay-to-Win**: Fishcoin cannot purchase cats, heroes, insight passives, lives, or continues.
- **Cosmetics Are Stat-Neutral**: Skins, banners, and trails do not alter range, hitboxes, audio pitch cues, or projectile collision radiuses.
- **Resell Controls**: Cosmetic items can be sold back for 50% of their Fishcoin cost; Tricks can be sold back for 40%.
- **Accessibility Guarantee**: Colorblind mode, audio sliders, screen shake settings, and subtitle options are permanent game settings, never shop purchases.

### Earning Fishcoin

| Source | Fishcoin Awarded | Notes |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **First Map Clear** (Sunny / Alley / Night / Last Whisker / Bare Paw) | 50 / 80 / 110 / 150 / 200 | One-time medal reward per map per mode. |
| **Repeat Map Clear** | 10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 40 | Standard completion payout. |
| **Daily Alley Clear** | +30 bonus | First clear of the daily seeded challenge. |
| **Weekly Puzzle Clear** | +80 bonus | Clear of the weekly fixed-loadout puzzle. |
| **Account Level Up** | +15 per level | Awarded on every level advance (1–40). |
| **Achievements** | 20 to 100 | One-time milestone awards. |

*First Session Milestone*: Clearing Sunny rounds 1–40 plus reaching Account Level 10 yields approximately **185 Fishcoin**. The player can afford their first cosmetic (80–100), while their first Trick (250–300) requires a subsequent run.

## 2. Tricks (Pre-Equipped Match Actives)

Tricks are equippable tactical abilities unlocked permanently with Fishcoin. Players equip up to **2 Tricks** prior to entering a match (expandable to **3** via the Clowder Insight tree).

### Core Trick Rules & Power Ceiling
- **Power Ceiling Law**: A single trick activation **cannot deal more than 1,100 damage** (5% of the Rat Matriarch's maximum HP) and **cannot skip a wave or round**.
- **No Consumable Ammo**: Tricks are reusable assets governed by round-based cooldowns and in-match kibble activation fees.
- **Bare Paw Mode**: Tricks are completely disabled in Bare Paw mode.

## 4. Daily & Weekly Challenge Systems

- **Daily Alley**: A daily seeded combination of map, mode, and modifier. First clear of the day awards +30 bonus Fishcoin on top of repeat clear earnings.
- **Weekly Puzzle**: A handcrafted tactical puzzle with fixed loadout (restricted cat roster, locked hero, tricks disabled). First clear awards +80 Fishcoin and that week's featured shop cosmetic for free.
- **Zero FOMO**: All weekly puzzle cosmetics enter the normal shop catalog at standard prices four weeks later.
